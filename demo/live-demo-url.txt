Live demo: Supply Chain Assistant deployed on Railway.

Start at the dashboard, then explore:
- `/disruptions` for active events and shipment impact analysis
- `/fleet/idle` for available fleet assets
- `/coldchain` for temperature readings and breach severity
- `/ops-brief` for the AI operations summary
- `/track` to search sample shipments such as `TRK-00421`
